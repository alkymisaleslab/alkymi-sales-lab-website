WHEN SALES AREN'T IMPROVING

Your team is busy. What's holding sales back?

Your team may know the product and work hard, yet deals still do not move. Maybe they are missing what buyers really need. Maybe managers have too little time to coach. Or maybe new skills fade once the workshop is over.

Each situation needs a different response. alkymi sales lab speaks with the people involved and looks at how your team sells before recommending training or changes to your sales system.

GOOD TO KNOW

A few common questions.

Here is what you can expect when we work together.

What does the diagnostic involve?+

The diagnostic is part of the agreed engagement. We interview the relevant people and, for training, observe mock pitches using your own business cases. We usually complete this within a week and share a report with recommended next steps.

What happens in the first conversation?+

We discuss your sales challenge, what you have already tried and what you want to improve. We can then discuss whether sales training or consulting fits what you need.

Is the self-check a formal assessment?+

No. It suggests questions to investigate. Assessing your team and business properly takes interviews and, where training is needed, observing the team in action.

What happens after the training diagnostic?+

We design a training module around your team's needs, then run workshops with practice and feedback followed by coaching. You receive a post-training report and a session to review the findings together.
